## Generative AI at Viatris

**Overview**

Viatris, a global healthcare company, is actively leveraging generative AI and broader AI technologies to enhance various aspects of its business, from drug discovery to internal operations.

**Key Use Cases**

- **Drug Discovery & Development**: Viatris employs AI to analyze large datasets (genomic, proteomic, clinical) to identify new drug candidates and optimize formulations, accelerating the drug development process and reducing costs[6].
- **Supply Chain Optimization**: AI-driven predictive analytics help forecast demand and optimize inventory, making the supply chain more efficient[6].
- **Patient Engagement**: AI chatbots and virtual assistants provide patients with personalized medication information and adherence support[6].
- **Pharmacovigilance**: Machine learning algorithms analyze adverse event reports to identify potential drug safety signals faster and more accurately[6].
- **Clinical Trials**: AI optimizes trial design, patient recruitment, and data analysis, improving trial efficiency and outcomes[6].
- **Personalized Medicine**: AI analyzes genetic and biomarker data to tailor treatments to individual patients[6].
- **Market Access & Fraud Detection**: AI is used to analyze market trends, pricing, and detect fraudulent activities such as counterfeit drugs[6].
- **Internal Productivity**: Generative AI tools, such as the Prezent platform, are used to automate the creation of on-brand presentations, saving time and ensuring consistency across the organization[7].

**Challenges**

- Data privacy, regulatory compliance, and ethical AI use are ongoing concerns. Viatris addresses these by investing in data governance and ethical frameworks[6].

**Conclusion**

Viatris is integrating generative AI across multiple business functions to drive innovation, improve patient outcomes, and streamline operations, while maintaining a focus on ethics and data security[6][7].
